HOW TO PLAY

Master the gravity, timing, and gap management to climb the ranks.

⌨️

Universal Controls

Desktop: Press SPACE, ↑ UP ARROW, or left-click anywhere.
Mobile & Tablet: Tap anywhere on the game screen.
Sound: Press M to toggle audio instantly.

⚙️

Authentic Physics

Every tap gives the bird an upward impulse of +1.55 velocity. Constant gravity of 5.0 m/s² pulls down with a maximum fall speed of 2.2. Maintain rhythmic tapping to stay level.

🛡️

Obstacle Navigation

Fly cleanly between the top and bottom blue metallic pipes. Each pipe passed successfully grants +1 point. Touching either pipe or crashing into the terrain ends the run.

ABOUT VASU ARCADE

An independent web gaming tribute built with passion for retro-arcade game design.

VASU ARCADE was conceived as a high-fidelity preservation and modernization showcase for classic arcade titles. Rather than rebuilding from approximations, our Flappy Bird edition is rooted in the authentic, reverse-engineered 120Hz physics engine of the original web release.

We set out to answer a clear design challenge: Can we give the legendary game a modern, cinematic arcade look without altering its iconic, razor-sharp gameplay mechanics? By mathematically preserving every collision boundary, jump impulse, and pipe gap while custom-crafting metallic blue pixel-art pipes, scenic mountain horizons, and dark neon chassis framing, VASU ARCADE brings the thrill back to modern screens.

Fixed 120Hz Physics

Runs on a deterministic simulation accumulator with sub-frame interpolation for buttery smoothness across 60Hz, 120Hz, and 144Hz monitors.

No Framework Bloat

Engineered using pure Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas 2D, and optimized Vanilla CSS. Instant loading with zero bundle overhead.

Authentic Audio

Integrated retro sound effects paired with real-time Web Audio API frequency synthesis as an instant fallback.

Privacy & Honest Records

No tracking scripts, no spam, no fake bots. All personal flight logs are saved honestly on your local device.
